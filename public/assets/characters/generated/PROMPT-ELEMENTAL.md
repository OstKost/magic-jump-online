Preserve ALL six character poses, identities, costumes, body sizes and positions, cell grid and transparent background. Bottom row three crouch/slide phases: a compact low wake hugging boots and extended leg, connected to the trailing hand. Match delicate ink and watercolor linework and crisp antialiased outlines of the character. Moderate decorative magic, keep face and limbs readable. Magic must stay within 35 pixels of the body in each 512px cell, no long tails; maintain 25 pixels empty padding to every cell boundary. No ground, text, shadows, grid lines, floating symbols, disconnected chunks, or extra characters. Do not modify the character geometry or put magic below the soles. Actual transparent RGBA background. Retain the existing large transparent upper margins in each cell; do not enlarge the characters.
